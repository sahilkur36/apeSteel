    Beam-Column Connections¶

    beam_column_connection ¶

    BeamColumnConnection - the joint composite.

    A :class:BeamColumnConnection aggregates two :class:Elements (a beam and a column) into one frozen object that exposes every joint-level seismic check as a method.

    Construction:

    1
2
3
4
5
    beam   = beam_section.element(material=A992, bracing=...)
column = column_section.element(material=A992)
joint  = beam.connected_to(column)
# or directly:
joint  = BeamColumnConnection(beam=beam, column=column)

    Usage:

    1
2
3
4
    cf_tension = joint.check_panel_zone_column_flange_tension()
pz_shear   = joint.check_panel_zone_shear()
doubler    = joint.recommend_doubler_plate()
cont       = joint.check_continuity_plates()

    This module lives at the top of src/apeSteel/ alongside element.py and bracing.py because connections are first-class domain objects, not internal subpackage members.

    BeamColumnConnection(beam: Element, column: Element)

    A frozen composite of (beam Element, column Element).
